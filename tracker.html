Who works on this, and what they may not do

The owner’s point on 30 Sep: the existing scheduled jobs are cron with a fixed prompt — the clock chooses when, and the work was chosen months ago. An agent picks its own next action. The difference is a backlog the agent owns.

Researcher — Mondays and Thursdays

  • Chooses its own workReads BACKLOG.md, takes the highest-value actionable item, does it, records the result and re-ranks. Prefers the test that could invalidate a belief over the one that could confirm it.
  • Cannot promote anythingMay register a threshold; may never move one. Nothing enters the book without published evidence, our own data, and forward confirmation at a threshold set before that data existed.
  • Silent unless it mattersNotifies only when a pre-registered hypothesis confirms, or when a current belief turns out wrong in a way that changes the book.

Watchdog — every weekday morning

  • Assumes the pipeline is lyingIt has failed silently four times — a network-dead run that exited 0, eight days of throttling that passed the health check, a cloud sync stall, and a smoke test that overwrote a day with 39 tickers. Every one looked fine from outside.
  • Names the mega-caps out loudMicrosoft, Alphabet, Meta and Tesla have each gone silently missing, every time found only because someone asked for one by name. That check is now standing.
  • Republishes the dashboardCron cannot. This is most of why it exists.

Why self-direction is fenced. The screen certifies 10% of pure noise. An agent free to pick its own research and promote its own findings would produce a confident false discovery roughly weekly — which is exactly how the previous loop generated dozens of candidates and dropped nearly all of them. The backlog lets it choose what to study; the two-source rule and pre-registered thresholds stop it choosing what to believe.

What this map is not

Coverage is not progress. Thirty-nine portfolio constructions were tested against SPY and none beat it — adding drivers did not move the result, and the two most recently added clusters changed nothing measurable. This map is a record of the search space, useful for knowing what has not been looked at. It is not a scoreboard, and a higher covered count would not mean a better system.

How to read the gaps

  • Buildable does not mean worth buildingEach still needs a mechanism and a boundary. The map is a search space, not a to-do list.
  • Unmeasurable is not unimportantManagement capital-allocation skill probably matters more than half the validated pool. It stays on the map so we never confuse what we can measure with what matters.
  • The roots are complete; the leaves are notEvery driver added in the last audit slotted into an existing root without forcing a new one. Future gaps will be more leaves, not a restructure.

Locked

  • Results are the goal, not method purityNo constraint on approach, high risk accepted. Hedge-fund underperformance is mostly fees, size and mandate — none of which bind here.
  • US-only universeEvery free alternative-data source is American; no other market has a structured equivalent.
  • Changes, not levelsPrices move on revised expectations. What everyone can screen for is already priced.
  • No funnelFour layers, each deciding a different thing. Macro sizes the book; it never picks names.
  • Condition inputs, not weightsAn indicator’s definition changes by company type. Its weight does not. Fifteen fitted parameters instead of 225.
  • Score carries skew, not just meanA composite built to rank average return systematically underweights fat-right-tail names — exactly the ones this portfolio wants. Tested 9 Oct: over 24 months the live 70/30 book earned +0.45%/mo vs SPY against +0.61%/mo for value alone (difference −0.16%/mo, t −0.20; random 30% overlays −0.30%, p=0.36). The skew term has not earned its place in backtest; it stays live until the forward record decides (threshold registered).
  • Position count floors at ~40Arithmetic. At a 5% hit rate, 10 names miss the winner 60% of the time.
  • Commission is zero; tax is the real costCommission is assumed zero and FX is not modelled, so per-trade cost is near zero — only bid-ask spread remains, 1–3bp in liquid names. The head start that annual-rebalance signals held over fast ones has gone. What is left is realisation tax, modelled at 42%: not a per-trade charge but a deferral cost, worth roughly 1.5–2pp a year. That is the entire hurdle a faster signal now has to clear.
  • Survivorship does not block a live scoreIt corrupts backtests, not today’s cross-section. This unblocked the whole build on free data.

Rejected — settled

  • Macro → sector → stock funnelGates kill the best names; errors compound; the weakest layer got the most attention.
  • Fitting weights per regime~225 free parameters on data that does not exist.
  • Options and marginOut of scope; margin is path-dependent ruin.
  • Pass/fail per signalAn earlier project’s design. Discards weak-but-real signals that only pay in a blend.
  • A self-improving loop, for nowThe architecture is right and the infrastructure already exists — it was never what failed. A loop amplifies whatever its evaluator measures, and a backtest at n=15 measures luck. Loop it once the evaluator is real.

How the build defends itself

Every signal gets an agent told to destroy it

Six checks each: lookahead, market-cap sanity, tail artefacts, coverage bias, sign direction, duplication. The builder had an incentive to report success. The verifier does not, and defaults to rejecting.

One check comes from experience: an earlier project produced a $4.1tn market cap for MDU Resources from a bad share-count join. Every verifier must now print the twenty largest companies and confirm they really are the largest before anything downstream is trusted.

One bet gets one vote

The payout cluster is eight separate measurements of a single idea — companies that fund themselves and return capital beat companies that keep raising money. Averaged within the cluster first, so it counts once.

Skip that step and the whole composite is one idea wearing a disguise, while looking diversified. This is the construction decision that matters more than any individual signal.
